M0016 FEATURE-ALIGNED MAP

This reconstruction was produced from the outbound image sequence using frame-to-frame
geometric registration. Neighboring frames were aligned using local image gradients and
Euclidean image transforms. Only after alignment were central image regions blended.

The output is a connected reconstruction of one underwater transect. It is not a complete
10 × 10 m orthophotoplan because the mission contained only one forward-and-return corridor.
Remaining softness is present in the original images due to turbidity, moving sunlight
patterns, focus limitations, and non-planar underwater refraction.
