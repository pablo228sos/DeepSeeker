# Mission M0014

M0014 was a long camera and telemetry run with 362 saved images. It proved that the acquisition pipeline could operate continuously, but only about 8% of frames passed a moderate local-detail threshold. Even the clearest continuous section could not support a valid metric orthophoto.

The main lesson was optical: speed and onboard stability did not explain the softness. Focus through the housing, water clarity, camera distance, window condition, and illumination needed attention before route refinements could matter.

See [`derived/M0014_analysis_report.md`](derived/M0014_analysis_report.md) for the complete diagnostic.
