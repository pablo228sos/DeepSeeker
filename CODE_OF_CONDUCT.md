# Code of conduct

DeepSeeker is an educational field-research project. We want discussions and contributions to be useful to students, researchers, and builders with different levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing another person's work.
- Discuss evidence, methods, and reproducible behavior rather than attacking people.
- Separate measured results from assumptions.
- Credit data, photographs, code, and ideas to their original contributors.
- Treat field safety and environmental responsibility as part of engineering quality.
- Help new contributors understand the system instead of dismissing basic questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Falsifying results, removing relevant failed trials, or presenting experimental output as validated measurement.
- Encouraging unsafe vehicle operation around people, boats, or wildlife.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove material, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
